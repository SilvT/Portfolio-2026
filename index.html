Meet Me!

Born and raised in the Canary Islands, now living in Barcelona.

Heavily shaped by British culture and humour.
I have more plants than surfaces and bake more focaccia than is reasonable per week.

Foundations

Architect → Graphic Designer → Product Designer → Design Technologist

Trained and worked as an architect before moving my structured foundations onto design.

Now I'm 5 years deep into design and design systems.

Since then I've found my niche on design systems and product design. Thinking in systems, tracing how one decision cascades down, and design for future complexities before they come into the table.

My background helps me face on complexity with a toolbelt heavy with problem-solving skills.

How I work

Systems, systems and then some more systems

✦ The product and the system go hand in hand - not one after it.

✦ Anyone can spot problems and frictions; what matters is coming up with the plan to face them.

✦ Early POC's and prototypes always.

✦ A solution will get you from A to B for now, but stay open for a better crossing further down the river.

✦ Sharing a language is TOP; befriend your developers. Know your users.

✦ A design is a living organism, is never finished, it will keep changing long after it reaches the client.
