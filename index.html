Meet Me!

Born and raised in the Canary Islands, now living between there and Barcelona. Heavily shaped by British culture and humour.
I have a lot of plants in my apartment and bake way too much focaccia weekly.

Foundations

Architect → Visual Designer → Product Designer → Design Technologist
+4.5 years across design and design systems.
I trained and worked as an architect before moving into design, and that’s stayed with me: thinking in systems, tracing how one decision constrains the next, and designing for failure before it happens.

Systems, systems and then some more systems

I build design systems alongside the product, not after it. If something’s going to grow, the structure has to be shaped early, even while things are still messy. I start loose and explore fast. Once direction forms, I tighten: naming, patterns, reduced friction, scaffolding.

My kind of vibe

Design systems aren't a trend for me. They're just how I think.
